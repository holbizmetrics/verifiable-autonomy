# Spec — Persona-Sweep Audit / "The Requirements Diff" (instance v0)

Locked 2026-07-29 per session dialog. Per `factory/SPEC.md` § Input "minimum viable
business-spec." **Same-shape sibling of `agent-audit-consulting` (audit-consulting
value→capture loop, content-parameterized). Explicitly NOT the factory's n≥2
structural-generalization test — see README § Shape disclosure.**

## 4 axes

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| What's sold | A requirements-coverage audit: the client's spec/feature list and user segments are swept against independently generated demand fragments (batched, with a measured saturation stopping rule), then diffed. Deliverable: (1) demands served by NO feature — the real roadmap gaps; (2) features touched by NO demand — named, killable speculation; (3) a segment-coverage ledger where every segment carries a recorded state, including *not-yet-examined*; (4) surprise measure — the client states their expected gaps at intake, and the report separates what they already knew from what the sweep surfaced beyond it |
| To whom | Starter ICP: mid-size B2B product teams with a shipped product and a contested backlog. Channel #1 is a warm PM contact (calibration run first, see Deferred) |
| How delivered | Async written report only (no call), markdown or PDF, 7 business days from payment confirmation |
| How captured | Email intake → manual invoice → sweep starts on payment confirmation. **$3,000 USD flat; full refund if the sweep surfaces zero gaps beyond the client's own pre-stated expected list.** |

## The refund condition IS the method's own bar

The client writes down their anticipated gaps at intake (the intake template forces it).
The sweep only earns its fee if it beats that frozen list — the same pre-registration
bar the method applies to itself. A client who could have written the report themselves
pays nothing. This makes the refund promise a *measurement*, not a marketing gesture,
and it prices exactly the thing the buyer cannot get from their own team: the
unanticipated.

## Why these starters

- **ICP starter:** warm-PM channel gives a real first loop faster than any cold page;
  mid-size B2B because their backlog fights are chronic and their spec artifacts exist.
- **Scope starter:** one product area per sweep (not the whole portfolio) caps effort
  and keeps the 7-day promise honest.
- **Price starter:** $3,000 flat, defensible against the deliverable being two lists +
  a ledger the org acts on for a quarter. Refine after sweeps 1–3.
- **Payment starter:** email intake + manual invoice, same as the sibling instance —
  capture mechanism proven there transfers here unchanged.

## Deferred (named, not done)

- **Calibration run #0** — the warm PM contact's own run is free-for-data (it is a
  calibration and a testimonial, not the paying loop). The PAID loop starts at sweep #1.
- **Report template** — hand-written after sweep #1; templated after 2–3 produce a shape.
- **Cross-generator premium tier** (multiple independent AI vendors sweeping the same
  packet) — only after first paying customer.
- **Tooling/SaaS form** — a different instance entirely; not this one.

## Done-condition (per factory/SPEC.md)

The instance deploys AND the value→capture loop works end-to-end — a test customer
(operator) can land → submit intake → receive invoice → get confirmation. **First real
customer = OUT-OF-SESSION, operator-bound.** The builder stops at "deployable +
loop-verified."
