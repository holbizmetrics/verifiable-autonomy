# Persona-Sweep Audit — intake

Reply to {{OPERATOR_EMAIL}} with the following. The sweep starts on payment
confirmation; the report lands within 7 business days.

1. **Product area under audit** (one area, not the portfolio): name + one paragraph.
2. **Your spec artifact** for that area: feature list, PRD, roadmap export — whatever
   form it exists in. Unpolished is fine; absent is not.
3. **Your user segments** as you currently name them (a list is enough).
4. **Your existing personas**, if any (optional — they are input, not the method).
5. **YOUR EXPECTED GAPS — required, and read this part carefully.** List the demands or
   segments you already suspect are underserved (3–10 bullets). This list is frozen at
   intake. The report will separate what you already knew from what the sweep found
   beyond it — **and if the sweep finds nothing beyond your list, you pay nothing.**
   An empty or vague expected-gaps list does not improve your refund odds; it is
   treated as "the team anticipates nothing," which is the easiest bar to beat.
6. **Constraints we must respect**: segments you deliberately do not serve (these enter
   the ledger as refused-on-purpose, with your stated reason), confidentiality bounds,
   anything we must not contact or crawl.
7. Invoicing details (company name, address, billing contact).
